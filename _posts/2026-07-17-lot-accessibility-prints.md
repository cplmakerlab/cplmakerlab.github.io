The HWLC has just launched their own Library of Things collection. In celebration of Disability Pride Month, we have 3D printed 6 different accessibility tools that are designed to make life easier for folks with grip strength issues. These items are bottle cap openers, pull tab can openers, jar openers, pen stabilizers, grocery bag holders, and book page holders.

If anyone has any questions about the collection, please feel free to contact our email at LibraryOfThings@chipublib.org
